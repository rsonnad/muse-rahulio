How the cheap collision cover works: the two cheapest options use a third-party reimbursement policy (Discover Cars Full Coverage / RentalCover at $13.20/day). The rental company can still charge you / hold the excess at the counter — you claim it back afterwards. The direct companies (Budget / Avis) sell their own LDW at about $33–38/day, which is why their all-in totals are much higher, but there is no claim-back step for covered damage.
