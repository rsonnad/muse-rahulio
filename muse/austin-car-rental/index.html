Tesla Model Y on Turo — taxes now included

Is Turo insurance mandatory? No. Per Turo's own US guest help page, a guest may pick a protection plan or decline — declining is allowed whether or not you have your own insurance, but then your responsibility for damage is not limited at all (full repair cost, or the car's cash value if written off). Turo's plans are not insurance; they cap what you pay out of pocket for damage to the host's car: Minimum — up to $3,000 out of pocket; Standard — up to $500. Premier ($0) was not offered on these cars. What IS included on every trip and cannot be declined: state-minimum third-party liability cover (Travelers, on an excess/secondary basis). Extra supplemental liability was not offered on these Texas checkouts and is not mandatory. Source: Turo — Protection plans in detail (US guests).
The grand totals below use the cheapest paid plan (Minimum), because that is the cheapest way to have any collision protection at all. Decline-protection and Standard totals are also shown.
