Taxes & fees — the full cost picture

Verified 2 Nov 2026 for 2025–2026 rules from Portal das Finanças–based guides, PwC Tax Summaries, CMS and specialist law-firm guides. Rates are set annually — confirm any deal in the AT (Autoridade Tributária) simulator / with a Portuguese lawyer before a deed. Full sources at the foot of this section.

⚠️ Critical 2026 change: from 25 May 2026, a buyer who is not a Portuguese tax resident pays a flat 7.5% IMT on urban residential property — no brackets, no exemptions (Decreto-Lei 97/2026). You can claim a refund of the difference vs resident rates if you become PT tax resident within 2 years, or let the home at a moderate rent ≤ €2,300/mo (lease signed within 6 months, kept ≥ 36 months in the first 5 years). This single rule swings a €620,000 purchase by about €10,700 — see the worked examples.

Renting in — what a tenant should (and should not) pay

ItemRule (in force since 1 Jan 2023)
Deposit (caução)Capped at 2 months' rent
Advance rentCapped at 2 months, and only if agreed in writing. With the first month, the lawful maximum at signing is 5 months' rent in total — demands for 6–12 months upfront exceed the caps (several listings above ask for guarantor + 2 + 2, which is within the caps)
Guarantor (fiador)Not required by law, but commonly requested in Lisbon, especially without PT employment / rental history. Negotiated alternatives: bank guarantee, rent-guarantee insurance, or the maximum lawful deposit + advance
Stamp duty on the lease10% of one month's rent — and it is the landlord's tax, paid when they register the contract on Portal das Finanças. No registration = no tenant IRS rent deduction (€900 cap in 2026, €1,000 in 2027) and a red flag. Since Aug 2025 the tenant can register it if the landlord fails
Term & risesFixed-term housing leases: minimum 1 year, auto-renewing. Annual rises are capped by the INE coefficient — +2.24% in 2026, +2.56% announced for 2027 — unless the contract provides otherwise
RepairsLandlord: structure, major repairs, essential systems, appliances supplied with the flat, lease registration, electronic rent receipts. Tenant: rent, utilities (and condo charges only if the lease says so), minor upkeep, damage from misuse

If you buy to let — landlord tax

ItemRule
Tax baseRental income (IRS Category F) is taxed on net income — IMI, condo charges, maintenance and insurance are deductible; mortgage interest is not
Flat rate, residential25% (28% now applies only to non-residential lets)
Long-lease reductionsLease 5–<10 yrs: 15% · 10–<20 yrs: 10% · ≥20 yrs: 5%. Possible further −5 points if a new ≥5-year lease is ≥5% below the previous rent on the same home
Moderate-rent rate (2026)10% until 31 Dec 2029 for residential rents ≤ €2,300/mo — which covers most Marvila long lets on this page, and pairs with the non-resident IMT refund condition above
Residents / non-residentsResidents may instead aggregate at progressive IRS rates if better. Non-residents are taxed in Portugal on PT rent at the same autonomous rates, need a NIF, and non-EU/EEA owners need a fiscal representative. Short-term tourist letting (Alojamento Local) is a different regime and is heavily restricted in Lisbon — do not model a long-let flat on AL income

Selling later — capital gains (brief)

Residents and non-residents alike: only 50% of the gain is taxable, added to income at progressive rates (effective rate on the full gain typically 0–24%). Deductible: the original IMT / stamp / notary, documented improvement works from the last 12 years, and sale costs / agent fees; the purchase price is inflation-indexed if held > 24 months. Reinvesting the proceeds in another primary residence in Portugal / EU / EEA within 36 months can exempt the gain in full or part.
