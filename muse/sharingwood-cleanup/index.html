What to do

  • Clear out: remove plastic sheeting and construction protection; take out leftover renovation dust and debris
  • Kitchen — deep clean: counters, cabinets, appliances, sink, and floors
  • Bathroom — deep clean: shower / tub, toilet, sink, mirror, and floors
  • All floors: vacuum, then mop
  • Dust everything: surfaces, baseboards, and window sills
  • Glass: clean windows and mirrors
  • Tidy + light staging: clear the counters, arrange furniture neatly, make the bed look fresh, put out fresh towels
  • Move furniture: some furniture may need moving around to arrange the rooms
  • Entry: make the first impression welcoming
  • Inspect & photograph problems: as you go, check the renovation work for anything broken, unfinished, damaged or not working — and take pictures of every problem you find
  • Photos: take before-and-after photos and send them when done, including the problem photos
